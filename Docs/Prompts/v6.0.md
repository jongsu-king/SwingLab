- Spin Axis
- Backspin Component
- Lateral Spin Component

## Technical Limitation

Do not assume normal iPhone footage permits accurate spin measurement.

A fast-moving golf ball can rotate too quickly for its surface details to be resolved reliably in ordinary high-speed video.

Temporal aliasing, motion blur, insufficient spatial resolution, and unknown viewing geometry may prevent reconstruction.

## Experimental Analysis

Investigate:

- Ball surface feature visibility
- Natural texture tracking
- Frame-to-frame rotation
- Rotation aliasing
- Angular velocity observability
- Spin-axis observability
- Reliability assessment

Do not require special marked golf balls.

If the rotation cannot be measured with adequate confidence, return:

Spin RPM: Unavailable

Do not turn ball curvature into an arbitrary RPM value.

Distinguish observed shot shape from measured spin.

Experimental measurements must remain separate from validated production features.
