## 2. Task B — review the reviewer's code (as you did with S1–S10 in Round 3)

Scope: every file listed under "Reviewer work" in `Docs/Review/round-4-review.md`, plus their tests.

Write `Docs/Review/round-5-review.md` as numbered findings **U1…**. Each finding needs:
- severity (Critical / High / Medium / Low);
- `file:line`;
- a concrete failure scenario (inputs or device conditions → wrong output, crash, or misleading UI);
- the fix you propose.

Look hardest at:
1. **Orientation.** The live path assumes rear-camera buffers arrive in landscape and turns them upright with a clockwise quarter turn (`LumaRotation.clockwise`). Check this against Apple's documentation for `AVCaptureVideoDataOutput` connections with `videoRotationAngle = 0`.
2. **Clocks.** Audio and video data-output timestamps are compared directly. Confirm they share the session's clock.
3. **Coexistence.** Can an `AVCaptureVideoDataOutput` and an `AVCaptureAudioDataOutput` run beside an `AVCaptureMovieFileOutput` at 240 fps on current iPhones? What happens when they can't?
4. **Concurrency and memory.** `LiveLaunchCapture` and `CameraEngine` are `@unchecked Sendable` with queue confinement. The ring holds about 48 MB at 240 fps.
5. **False triggers.** Practice swings, a neighbouring golfer's strike, and mat or tee sounds.
6. **Honesty.** Every displayed launch value must keep its provenance. The carry range is simulated and has a known bias (`Docs/Specs/LaunchMonitor.md`, "Known gap").

Fix only Critical and High findings, after writing them up. The reviewer answers the rest in a rebuttal.
